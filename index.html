01 / about the event

Where ideas become impact

SheSolves 2026 brings together students, builders and curious minds to see what computing can actually do when women lead the room.

A working day for women in computing.

SheSolves 2026 is an ACM-W PCCOE initiative that gives women the room to explore technology, pitch rough ideas out loud, and turn real-world problems into something that actually runs.

Whether you're writing your first line of code or already shipping projects, you'll leave with something built, a team you trust, and a reason to keep going.
